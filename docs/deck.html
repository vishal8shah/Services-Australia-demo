The gap 03

Two vocabularies that never meet, until something translates between them

What a person types
“Mum is 82…”
“…moving in with us…”
“…dropped to three days a week”
query expansion
What the pages say
constant care term
care receiver term
Carer Payment payment
income test rule

None of these words appear on the pages, so matching on spelling finds nothing.

One small model call rewrites the situation into the pages' own terms. Evidence found this way counts for less.

What it refuses 06

It knows what not to answer, and where to send you instead

Record

Your claim or balance

That needs a login, and this tool holds no personal data at all.

→ myGov
Amounts

How much you'll get

Rates change and depend on your circumstances. A wrong figure hurts most when money is tight.

→ official estimator
Time

Wait and processing times

Operational data we don't have, and must not seem to have.

→ the phone line
Decision

“Do I qualify?”

We show eligibility signals. Only the agency decides.

→ the payment's own page
Identifiers

CRN, TFN, Medicare, birth date

Blocked before search runs, and the person is told why.

→ nothing is stored
Scope

Business and provider topics

A different part of the site, and not in this tool's content.

→ the right entry point

How it stays honest 08

Six checks run after the model, in code, not left to the prompt

RULE 1

No invented sources

Every cited id was actually retrieved for this question

RULE 2

Nothing unsourced

Every eligibility signal and next action cites a source

RULE 3

No invented payments

Each payment name appears word for word in a passage it cites

RULE 4

Links only to cited pages

A link that doesn't match a cited source is removed

RULE 5

Right shape or nothing

If the JSON is malformed, retry once, then refuse

RULE 6

No money or time figures

A dollar figure or wait time is removed and listed as not answered

Rule 3does most of the work: the model can't name a payment the pages never mention.

The value 11

Who gains, and how

People

Start from their life, not a payment name

  • Every payment that applies to a compound situation, in one answer
  • Answers in their own language
  • Source links they can show to anyone
The agency

People reach the official page, not a phone queue

  • Every answer sends traffic to its own pages
  • Could take pressure off calls that only need the right page
  • No personal data, and every claim traces to a dated page
Delivery teams

A pattern worth reusing

  • Fixed answer shape, checks in code, refusals designed in
  • Quality shown by a scorecard, not by how the demo feels
  • Page dates flag stale content at no extra cost
Reduction in calls is a hypothesis to test, not a result
